# Hero Copy Decisions

## Why "Know" instead of "Understand"?
- **Understand** reads passive and academic.
- **Know** is immediate and actionable (“I want to know who my reps are and what they did.”).
- For the NYC persona (Sofia), the job-to-be-done is clarity and verification, not civic education.

## Why "NY representatives" instead of "your representatives"?
- **Specificity** makes the product feel real and locally relevant (NYC-based user).
- **Personalization** signals “this is for me” without adding location infrastructure yet.
- **Trust**: geo-specific framing implies concrete, grounded data.

## Why remove "Make informed choices"?
- It’s implied by “See how they vote.”
- Heroes should be scannable in <3 seconds; reducing a whole line increases comprehension.

## Why "Real records. Real impact."?
- “Real records” is the core trust claim (votes/records, not vibes).
- “Real impact” ties the data to the user’s life, without over-explaining.
- Two short assertions beat a longer list; it reads confident instead of salesy.

## Accessibility notes
- Headline stays **two explicit lines** via block spans (no layout hacks).
- `aria-label` mirrors the headline for clean screen reader announcement.
